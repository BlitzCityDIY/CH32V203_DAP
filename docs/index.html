GNDGND

            
A0 (PB1)nRESET (optional)

          

          
Power the target from its own USB or battery. Keep the SWD wires short.

        

        

          
Requirements

          
Chrome, Edge or another Chromium browser on a desktop computer. Firefox and Safari don’t support WebUSB.

          
Only one program can use the QT Py at a time: close OpenOCD, pyOCD or other tabs first.

          
On Linux, give your user access to the QT Py with a udev rule (see the README).

          
Targets: STM32F4 family, first 1 MB of flash.
